# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space, and to anyone taking
part.

## Expected

- Treat people with respect, including when you think they are wrong
- Accept that reasonable people disagree about design, and that maintainers make final calls
- Give feedback about code and behaviour, not about character
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, and move on

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption
- Demeaning, discriminatory, or personal remarks — including about someone's screen-time habits,
  their reasons for wanting this app, or anything they disclose while reporting a bug
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sexual attention or imagery in project spaces

## A note specific to this project

NoScroll is used by people trying to change habits they are not proud of, and some of them use the
explicit-content filter. Reports may touch on that.

**Treat anything a person discloses about their own use with complete discretion, and never mock it.**
If somebody reports a false positive and mentions a category of content, respond to the bug. That is
the whole interaction.

This is also why the app deliberately stores no browsing history, no search terms, and no account
identity: there should be nothing to disclose in the first place. Please do not ask a reporter for
information the app was designed not to keep.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled discreetly.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban participants for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, and will explain the reasoning where doing so does not compromise someone's privacy.
